This page captures the screen, encodes it with the GPU, and hands the encoded frames to a local relay that publishes to the same live path OBS uses. No second encode and no WebRTC ceiling. It runs only on the host laptop.

Defaults match the OBS profile: 1920×1080, 24 fps, CBR 4 Mbps, 1 s keyframes. Keep the small On Air window open while you broadcast — Chrome throttles hidden tabs, and that window keeps the encoder at full speed when you switch away.
